Square, flat CTA for briefs, proposals and carousel end-cards ("Submit your request here", "Reach out to us →").
```jsx
<Button variant="primary" arrow>Request your expert list</Button>
<Button variant="inverse">PROPOSAL · PREPARED FOR PIBIT</Button>
```
- `variant`: primary (purple), dark (near-black bar, used on Expert Voices end-cards), outline, inverse (white frame on aurora), ghost (underlined link).
- `size`: sm | md | lg. `arrow` adds a trailing →. Never rounded.
