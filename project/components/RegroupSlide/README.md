```jsx
<RegroupSlide n={14} eyebrow="AI / Tech" title="AI MVP: Europe data acquisition complete">{/* content */}</RegroupSlide>
```
- Regroup is the one surface where the system loosens up: icons (`RegroupIcon`), status pills, confetti, photos and leaderboards are welcome here and only here. Corners stay square, no shadows, no emoji.
- 90px side margins. Keep body text at 16px or larger: no screenshots of charts with tiny labels, rebuild them with `RegroupStats`.
