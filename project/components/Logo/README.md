The official Infoquest lockup — top-left of cover/header bands, bottom-right of carousel covers and CTAs.
```jsx
<Logo height={32} assetBase="../../assets/" />  {/* white, for aurora */}
<Logo variant="color" height={28} />
<Logo markOnly height={40} />
```
- `color` on white/light pages; `white` (default) on the aurora and photography; `inverse` only on solid near-black. The purple roundel disappears on the aurora — never use color/inverse there.
- High-res PNG (1600px wide, transparent). Never recolor, stretch or put the full-color lockup on mid-tones.
